# Trading Strategies & Best Practices

## 🎯 Understanding Trading Strategies

### Conservative Strategy
**Goal**: Steady, low-risk profits
- Lower position sizes (1-2% per trade)
- Fewer trading pairs (3-5 major coins)
- Higher sell targets (1.5-2%)
- Stricter stop losses (-15%)

### Balanced Strategy  
**Goal**: Moderate risk/reward
- Medium position sizes (2-3% per trade)
- Moderate pairs (5-7 coins)
- Balanced sell targets (2-3%)
- Reasonable stop losses (-20%)

### Aggressive Strategy
**Goal**: Higher profits, higher risk
- Larger position sizes (3-5% per trade)
- More trading pairs (7-10 coins)
- Higher sell targets (3-5%)
- Wider stop losses (-25%)

## 📊 Technical Indicators Explained

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- **Oversold**: RSI < 30 (good buy signal)
- **Overbought**: RSI > 70 (good sell signal)
- **Conservative**: Buy at RSI 40, sell at RSI 60
- **Aggressive**: Buy at RSI 35, sell at RSI 65

### EMA Cross Strategy
- **Buy Signal**: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA
- **Sell Signal**: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA
- **Common Settings**: 12/26 or 9/21 periods

### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
- **Buy Signal**: MACD line crosses above signal line
- **Sell Signal**: MACD line crosses below signal line
- **Momentum**: Histogram shows trend strength

## 💰 Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)

### What is DCA?
DCA automatically buys more of a coin as its price drops, lowering your average buy price.

### DCA Settings:
- **Trigger**: -2% (start DCA when coin drops 2%)
- **Max buys**: 5 levels maximum
- **Buy amounts**: Increase with each level (100%, 150%, 200%, etc.)

### DCA Example:
1. Initial buy: 0.001 BTC at $50,000
2. Price drops to $49,000 (-2%): Buy 0.0015 BTC
3. Price drops to $48,000 (-4%): Buy 0.002 BTC
4. Average cost is now lower, easier to profit

## 🛡️ Risk Management

### Position Sizing
- **Never risk more than 5% of portfolio per trade**
- **Start with 1-2% position sizes**
- **Increase gradually as you gain experience**

### Stop Losses
- **Conservative**: -15% stop loss
- **Aggressive**: -25% stop loss
- **DCA positions**: -35% stop loss

### Diversification
- **Don't put all funds in one pair**
- **Mix large caps (BTC, ETH) with altcoins**
- **Consider market correlation**

## 📈 Market Conditions

### Bull Market
- Use aggressive strategies
- Higher sell targets
- More trading pairs
- Wider stop losses

### Bear Market
- Conservative approach
- Lower position sizes
- Fewer pairs
- Tighter stop losses

### Sideways Market
- Scalping strategies
- Quick profits (1-2%)
- High frequency trading
- Technical analysis focus

## 🔧 Configuration Tips

### Pair Selection
**Safe pairs**: BTC, ETH, BNB, ADA, DOT
**Medium risk**: LINK, UNI, MATIC, AVAX
**Higher risk**: Smaller altcoins (research first)

### Timeframes
- **Scalping**: 1-5 minute candles
- **Day trading**: 15-60 minute candles  
- **Swing trading**: 4-24 hour candles

### Backtesting
Always test strategies on historical data before live trading:
1. Use PT Feeder or similar tools
2. Test multiple market conditions
3. Verify risk/reward ratios
4. Adjust parameters as needed

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

1. **Over-leveraging**: Using too much capital per trade
2. **FOMO trading**: Chasing pumps without strategy
3. **Ignoring stop losses**: Letting losses run too long
4. **Too many pairs**: Spreading focus too thin
5. **Emotional trading**: Making decisions based on fear/greed
